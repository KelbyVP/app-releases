# Privacy Policy — Kelby's Christmas

**Last updated:** 2026-08-05  
**Operator:** Kelby Van Patten (`kelbyvp@gmail.com`)  
**App:** Kelby's Christmas (Android package `com.kelbyvp.kelbyschristmas`; Windows desktop)

This policy describes how the Kelby's Christmas app handles information. The app is invite-oriented for family use, not a public social network.

## What the app does

Kelby's Christmas helps households manage Christmas wish lists, shopping claims, budgets, and family “circles.” It runs on Windows and Android and can sync across devices when you create an account.

## Account information

- You may register with a **display name and password** (no email is required for the in-app name/password path).
- We store your account record on a **PocketBase** server we operate for this app (`christmas-pb.rock-my-retirement.com`).
- Passwords are stored as secure hashes by PocketBase; we do not receive or store plaintext passwords after registration.

## Wish lists, claims, and family data

When you use the app while signed in, the following may be stored on the server and on your devices:

- Wish items you create (titles, notes, links, ratings)
- Group membership (“circles”), shares, and grants that control who can view a list
- Shopping claims and prices you enter for gifts you buy for others
- Budget planned/spent figures you enter
- Optional gift-size preferences you save on your account

**Giftee privacy:** the product is designed so the person receiving gifts does not see who claimed or bought items intended for them. Access to shopping state is limited by grants and roles enforced on the server and in the client.

Local-only data (contacts, some notes) stays on the device and is not uploaded to PocketBase.

## Device data

On each device the app may store:

- Local SQLite databases for your lists and shopping state
- Session tokens and preferences (including “stay signed in”)
- Avatar choice for your account on that device
- Optional PIN for device unlock / little-kid mode

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not show third-party advertising in the app.
- We do not require a public social profile.
- We do not claim end-to-end encryption of server-stored data (data is protected by account auth, access rules, and transport TLS).

## Children

The app supports little-kid / child modes managed by adults in the family. Children’s accounts and lists are intended for family coordination. Do not create accounts for children without parental permission where required by law.

## Sharing with others

Data is shared only as you configure in the app (for example, members of a circle you join, or a partner budget view). Server access rules enforce those choices. Operators of the PocketBase instance can access data as needed to run backups and support the family deployment.

## Backups and retention

Server data is backed up periodically for recovery. You may soft-delete wishes and leave groups; residual claim snapshots may remain for shoppers who already purchased for someone.

## Updates and distribution

Installs from **Google Play** or the **Microsoft Store** update through those stores. Sideload installs may check a public release channel (`KelbyVP/app-releases`) for optional updates.
